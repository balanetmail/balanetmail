# Personal website content

- Match the site's existing tone and writing style.
- Write in first person, unless the page already uses another voice.
- Keep the writing clear, specific, and concise.
- Do not invent jobs, skills, achievements, or personal details. Ask me when needed.
- Before editing, inspect the relevant page and follow its existing layout.
- After editing, summarize what changed.
